One place for your whole team

A blank canvas for the way your company works.

You put in what matters to your team, and everyone can find it when they need it. You decide what goes in and who sees it.

Sales

Your sales process, your sales training, your playbooks, and anything else your reps need before and during a call.

Service

Your procedures, your job checklists, your tech training, and the code books and specs your techs rely on.

Managers

Your training decks, ready to present at the morning meeting, plus the documents you use to run training and coaching.

Everyone

Your policies, your team directory and important numbers, and a home page with your company news.

A rep sees what applies to sales and a tech sees what applies to service. On a job, tap the job type and everything you've tagged for it comes up at once. To help you start, UpSwing includes a few templates, like sample checklists and ride-along score cards, that you can use, change or delete. Everything else is yours.

Ongoing training

The hard part isn't the first 30 days.

Getting a new hire up to speed is the easy part, and UpSwing gives them a 30-day plan that runs itself. The hard part is everything after: keeping a five-year tech sharp, rolling out a new process, and making sure March's training still shows up in June.

That's where most training quietly dies. UpSwing keeps it alive.

Assign it and see it get done

Pick a course, pick the people, set a due date. See who finished and who's behind without chasing anyone. Reminders go out on their own.

Training that renews itself

Courses that need doing every year reopen on their own before they expire, with a record of every time someone finished.

A five-minute weekly refresher

Every Monday, each person gets a few quick questions from what they've already learned, weighted toward what they've missed before.

Questions answered once

People ask right on the training. Answer it once and the whole team sees the answer.

Make every ride-along count

You can't ride with everyone every week. Score cards rate each step, track each person over time, and flag the steps your whole team is weakest on. Your lead techs and reps can run ride-alongs too, so coaching doesn't wait on your calendar.

Sales dashboard

Training and sales numbers, side by side.

UpSwing comes with a sales dashboard built for the way home services teams sell. Enter jobs as they close and everyone sees where they stand against goal for the week, month and year.

Set yearly goals once and the dashboard spreads them across your busy and slow seasons. Show any rep what an extra point of close rate or a bigger average sale would do to their number. Put the board on the shop TV and let it run.

Because training and sales live in the same place, you can see whether the rep who finished the course is closing more, and spot who needs coaching before the numbers drop.
