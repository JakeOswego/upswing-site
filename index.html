Ongoing training

The hard part isn't the first 30 days.

Getting a new hire up to speed is the easy part, and UpSwing gives them a 30-day plan that runs itself. The hard part is everything after: keeping a five-year tech sharp, rolling out a new process, and making sure March's training still shows up in June.

That's where most training quietly dies. UpSwing keeps it alive.

Assign it and see it get done

Courses that need doing every year reopen on their own before they expire, with a record of every time someone finished.

Questions answered once

Sales dashboard

Training and sales numbers, side by side.

UpSwing comes with a sales dashboard built for the way home services teams sell. Everyone sees where they stand against goal for the week, month and year.

No double entry: export your sales report from ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber or any spreadsheet and drop it in. Columns and names are remembered, and change orders and duplicates are caught, so keeping it current takes about a minute.

Set yearly goals once and the dashboard spreads them across your busy and slow seasons. Show any rep what an extra point of close rate or a bigger average sale would do to their number. Put the board on the shop TV and let it run.

Because training and sales live in the same place, you can see whether the rep who finished the course is closing more, and spot who needs coaching before the numbers drop.

Questions

What owners ask us

How do our sales numbers get in?

Export your sales report from ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber or any spreadsheet and drop it in. UpSwing reads the columns, matches names to your people, catches change orders and skips anything already imported. It remembers your report, so after the first time it takes about a minute. For close rates that match your CRM exactly, type each person's appointments and sold for the week; that's a couple of minutes. Import daily if you want the shop TV current every morning, or weekly if that's enough. Direct CRM connections are on the way.

What happens on a job with no cell signal?

Before heading into a basement or a dead zone, techs tap "Save for offline" on the checklists, specs and manuals they'll need, and those open on the phone with no signal. Courses, quizzes and anything that saves progress need a connection and pick up where they left off when they're back in range.

Our techs aren't big on technology. Will they actually use it?

It goes on their home screen like any app, no app store needed. It opens on their day: the next course to finish, anything due, policies to sign. On a job, one tap on the job type brings up everything for it. New training sends a notification, and the weekly refresher is a few questions that take five minutes. If someone falls behind, you see it in the Monday summary.

What do we need for setup, and how long does it take?

Send whatever you have: PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files, spreadsheets, videos or YouTube links, photos, even rough notes. With our setup help we build your first courses, quizzes, checklists and onboarding plans, usually within about two weeks. Prefer to do it yourself? Every company starts with a few templates and a getting-started checklist.
